Prevention & Wellbeing

Fire prevention, legionella, first aid.
Finally under control.

Every hotelier knows the feeling: somewhere in a cupboard there's a binder with inspections, checklists and safety plans. Nobody knows if everything's still valid. Until an inspector shows up. rumo replaces that cupboard with a living system that thinks ahead, reminds and documents.

  • Fire prevention file: extinguishers, detection, emergency lighting. With expiry dates and automatic reminders
  • Legionella management plan: temperature readings, flushes and logbook. Always inspection-ready
  • Plan, register and evaluate evacuation drills with your team
  • Track first aid supplies and AEDs: who's trained, what expires when
  • Register chemical products with safety data sheets and storage locations
  • Daily and periodic checklists your team fills in on the floor, with photos and PDF export
  • Incident reports and near-misses: everyone reports, managers follow up
  • Contractor registration: external parties register themselves, sign safety rules

And beyond

Everything you'd expect from a PMS.
And maybe a little more.

The basics are all there too. Every single one.

Reservations & Calendar

Visual calendar with drag-and-drop. Multi-room bookings, per-room status, quotes and full booking history.

Channel Manager

Booking.com, Airbnb, Expedia and more. Prices and availability are automatically synced via Channex.

Booking Engine

Direct bookings through your own website, commission-free. With referrer tracking and allotments for partners.

Invoicing & Peppol

Invoices, credit notes and proformas with correct VAT. Peppol e-invoicing and UBL 2.1 XML export built in.

Payments

Online payment links, VCC charging, pre-authorizations. Bancontact, Visa, Mastercard. Automatically linked to invoices.

Guest Portal

Guests check in online themselves, confirm quotes, pay and fill in their ID details. On their own phone.

Housekeeping

Real-time room status. Cleaning staff works from their phone, updates appear instantly in the calendar.

F&B

Breakfast lists, minibar and restaurant charges. Automatically added to the room bill, linked to the rate plan.

Communication

Email templates per event, sent automatically in your guest's language. Multilingual: NL, FR, DE, EN.

Rates & Allotment

Room types, rate plans, cancellation policies and allotments for partners. Pushed to all channels.

Reports

Occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, revenue per channel. Central invoice overview. Export to CSV, Excel or PDF.

Guest Profiles

Automatically built from reservations. Guest, booker and company profiles with VIES VAT validation.
