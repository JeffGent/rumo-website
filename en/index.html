Who rumo works for

For your team.
And for you.

Running a hotel is a lot of things at once. There's a guest waiting at the desk. Someone in the breakfast room is asking about gluten-free. Housekeeping wants to know which rooms are leaving early. And tonight you still need to review those rates, check that report, hope the new hire is ready for tomorrow.

That's why rumo is three parts: the PMS that runs your day, the handbook your team can search themselves, and Rudy who keeps going when you step away.

The PMS

For receptionists. Not for IT people.

You open rumo in the morning. Who's arriving, who's leaving, who hasn't paid yet, which room hasn't been cleaned. One screen. No three tabs, no "hang on, I need to open that other site first".

The handbook

For your team. Without them calling you.

How does the espresso machine work? Where's the vacuum? What about gluten-free? How do I silence that alarm after a false trigger? Your team looks it up. Not in a fifty-person group chat. Not with a phone call on Sunday night.

Rudy

Works at night. Works Sundays. Works for nothing.

An AI assistant that knows both the PMS and the handbook. Ask him to look up a reservation, summarise a procedure, reply to a guest in German, run a report. And he never asks for a raise.

Question or doubt?

The six questions
we often get.

Can I migrate my Booking.com reservations?

Yes. Booking.com, Airbnb, Expedia and most other OTAs can be imported directly. Including guest details and payment status.

Does rumo work on mobile and tablet?

Fully. rumo is a web app that runs anywhere — laptop, tablet, phone. No app to install.

How long does implementation take?

Most hotels are live within a week. Small hotels often the same day.

What if I'm still in a contract with my current PMS?

No problem. We plan the switch at your pace. Many hotels start testing while the old contract is still running out.

Is Peppol included?

Peppol is an upgrade in rumo. With the upgrade, your invoices are sent automatically via the Peppol network (UBL 2.1). Without the upgrade, you just create invoices in rumo and download them as PDF or UBL file.

Can I cancel monthly?

You start with an initial six-month period. After that, month to month, cancel whenever you want. No fine print, no notice period.
