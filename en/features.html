And more

Everything you'd expect from hotel software.
And maybe a bit more.

The basics are all in there too. One by one.

Housekeeping

Real-time room status. Cleaning staff works from their phone, updates show up immediately on the calendar.

F&B

Breakfast lists, minibar and restaurant charges. Automatically on the room bill, linked to the rate plan.

Messaging

Direct messages between your team. Front desk to housekeeping, owner to restaurant. No more WhatsApp group where nobody can find anything.

rumo pay

Online payment links, card charging, pre-authorisations. Bancontact, Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay. Automatically linked to invoices.

Invoicing & Peppol

Invoices, credit notes and proformas with correct VAT. Downloading as PDF or UBL 2.1 is included. Sending over the Peppol network is an upgrade.

Reports

Occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, revenue per channel. Central invoice overview. Export to CSV, Excel or PDF.

Belgian compliance

City tax per municipality, police registration and government statistics (Statbel in Belgium, the lodging survey in the Netherlands). All automatic, based on what your guest filled in. Set it up once, then just let it run.

Allotments

Fixed room blocks for tour operators, corporate or partners. With pickup tracking and central billing. Sold or not, you've got the overview.

Maintenance

Broken lock on room 12? Leaking tap in the kitchen? Anyone logs an issue with a photo. You see what's pending, in progress, coming up. No more post-its at the front desk.
