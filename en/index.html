Question or doubt?

The six questions
that everyone asks.

Can I migrate my Booking.com reservations?

Yes. Booking.com, Airbnb, Expedia and most other OTAs can be imported directly. Including guest data and payment status.

Does rumo work on mobile and tablet?

Fully. rumo is a web app that runs everywhere. Laptop, tablet, phone. No app needed.

How long does implementation take?

Most hotels are live within a week. Smaller hotels often the same day.

What if I'm still in a contract with my current PMS?

No problem. We plan the switch at your pace. Many hotels start testing while the old contract is still running out.

Is Peppol included?

Peppol is available in rumo as an upgrade. With the upgrade, your invoices are automatically sent via the Peppol network (UBL 2.1). Without the upgrade, you simply create your invoices in rumo and download them as PDF or UBL files.

Can I cancel monthly?

You start with an initial period of six months. After that, month by month, cancelable whenever you want. No fine print, no notice period.
